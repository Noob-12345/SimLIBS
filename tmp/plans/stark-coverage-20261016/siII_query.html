How to use

Below are the equations used to get the fitted data :

  • log(w)=a_0 + a_1 log(T) + a_2 (log(T))^2
  • d/w = b_0 + b_1 log(T) + b_2 (log(T))^2

N.B : This fitting must only be used inside a temperature interval where widths and/or shifts are provided.

Reference : S. Sahal-Bréchot, M. S. Dimitrijević , N. Ben Nessib , “Comparisons and comments on electron and ion impact profiles of spectral lines”, 2011, Baltic Astronomy, Vol. 20, pp. 523-530
